# BlindIQ v1.71.0 — native hunt sharing

Saved sessions and logbook entries now use the same share preview dialog. BlindIQ prepares a 1080×1350 PNG before enabling Share to apps. A fresh tap invokes `navigator.share({files:[file]})` synchronously, avoiding the loss of user activation caused by downloading photos or generating a canvas after the share tap. Caption text is separate: hunters can edit and copy it before opening the device share sheet, then paste it into their chosen app. Image-only payloads avoid mixing caption/link data into an image handoff.

The operating system chooses compatible installed share targets. BlindIQ cannot enumerate apps or signed-in social accounts, select Instagram/Facebook on the user's behalf, or confirm that a post was published. The social app uses its own current login and provides the final posting controls. No social OAuth, passwords, tokens, third-party posting service or public photo upload is added. A resolved share promise is described only as a handoff, never proof of a published post. Physical iOS/Android app target availability still requires real-device validation.

Cancel and sharing failures never download automatically. Unsupported browsers offer explicit Save image and Copy caption; blocked clipboard access selects the editable caption for manual copying. The preview is a native HTML dialog with keyboard focus containment, Escape/Close and responsive sizing. Signed-in private photos are fetched locally and encoded into the card; the card isn't uploaded by BlindIQ. Failed photo loading is explicit, with retry or an opt-out to prepare a card without the photo. Generated cards strip image metadata through canvas encoding. Area/blind details are omitted by default and can be included after review. Notes and location-weather coordinates are not added to captions. A neutral branded background replaces the duck-only fallback for hunts without photos.

Source: https://www.w3.org/TR/web-share/ and https://webkit.org/blog/13862/the-user-activation-api/ (fresh transient activation and device-controlled share targets).

Validation: 81 unit/database tests; mobile browser checks for preview dimensions, caption counts/privacy, fresh-gesture image-only calls from current hunts and logbook, cancellation, policy errors, unsupported sharing, explicit downloads, clipboard fallback, photo failure/recovery, Escape and four viewport widths. Native methods were mocked; no real social posts were sent. Existing login/deletion, logger and installed-cache upgrade/offline checks pass. Production build guards retain map exclusion and matching app/cache versions.
