# BlindIQ v1.61 — 2026–2027 waterfowl regulation audit

Review date: **November 11, 2026 (America/New_York)**. Baseline: `zach842/BlindIQ_MAIN_2`, `main`, commit `d8876d82c2b2a3411dd8340b7cb292e297a73b03`, package version 1.60.0.

This is an incremental update of the existing application. The 29 states, existing navigation, accounts, subscriptions, weather, Migration Pulse, notifications, offline support, photos, sharing and saved-hunt formats remain. No database migration or application replacement is included. The legacy `BlindIQ-ID-OR-WA-v1.23 2` folder remains untouched; the active application is the repository root.

## Verification scope and result

All 29 state packages were reviewed against agency publications or their official regulation-guide publisher. The table distinguishes corrections, retained rules, and unresolved source conflicts. “Reviewed” means the listed material was checked; it is not a claim that every refuge, tribal restriction, permit condition or emergency closure has been independently certified.

**Source conflicts resolved using final material.** Illinois' web table reverses scaup limits in two zones; its final 2026–27 digest quick-reference card and the IDNR digest announcement agree on one for the first 15 open days, then two. South Dakota's general waterfowl page still says 15 early Canada geese; current codified rules 41:06:16:09 and 41:06:50:03 confirm eight, effective May 25, 2026. The final sources are linked below and take precedence in v1.61.

The logger calculates **bag space for the current hunt**, not legal permission to shoot. Some date restrictions now affect the calculation, but it does not model all species seasons, permits, special areas, shooting hours, season-long quotas, residency or eligibility. It does not combine separate hunts into one daily bag, track possession, or implement alternate Three-Duck/Tier II elections. Hunters must include earlier harvest and use their elected bag regime. The UI now says “Bag space remaining,” shows the calculation date, and explains this scope instead of asserting that birds may legally be harvested.

## Calculation changes and evidence

- Separate merganser and goose parent groups no longer inherit the duck-bag constraint. This fixes, for example, a five-merganser bag incorrectly closing after six ducks and a five-goose aggregate being capped by remaining duck space.
- Shared limits are resolved consistently across siblings, including zone caps. Unknown boundary selections use the lower loaded cap instead of a larger implicit default.
- Date rules now cover scaup transitions, specific closures, bonus teal and selected seasonal goose/wood-duck changes. Special-day eligibility is still a hunter responsibility. These rules do not constitute a complete species-season authorization engine.
- Add-bird validation runs against the latest React state, preventing rapid clicks from bypassing a filled cap.
- Logger, summary and current/saved share totals include all entries, including coots and separate mergansers. Duck and goose subtotals keep their respective categories. Historical saved classifications are not rewritten.
- Browser-local civil date drives bag guidance. An overseas or incorrectly configured device timezone is not corrected through geolocation. The app does not retrospectively recalculate historical hunts.
