# State reporting resource review — November 19, 2026

Scope: official agency reporting/registration/survey guidance or an agency-endorsed licensing resource for each of the 50 state choices. This directory does not assert that every species requires a report, that online reporting replaces physical checks, or that BlindIQ satisfies a legal harvest-record requirement. Follow the agency for current species, tag, permit and deadline rules. No nationwide deadline is hardcoded.

Verification: 41 resources responded to live HTTP fetches. Nine restricted automated fetches; their official indexed content was reviewed instead. Those restrictions are recorded, not represented as successful live checks. Old Indiana/Minnesota reporting paths and obsolete Ohio website paths were replaced; Ohio points to its current official hunting guide. Louisiana licensing support is an agency-endorsed vendor resource, supported by LDWF published licensing guidance.

The raw link/status/redirect audit is in [LINK_AUDIT_2026-11-19.json](LINK_AUDIT_2026-11-19.json). Source titles and species coverage are intentionally narrow where a page addresses only deer or a particular survey. The adjacent agency link provides a route to other species.
